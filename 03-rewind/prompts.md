# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
Break the numbers out by responder.  Did misses happen to everyone, or only specific responders?

### 2.
Run the weekly view of just the four

### 3.
Is there data with regards to time of day, geographical location, or other issues that would affect why these four had higher missed rates?

### 4.
What is time to accept ping for all both before and after the release date?

### 5.
What should I be asking to determine issue, and what is response?

### 6.
Are the four using different phones or equipment from the other 12?

### 7.
If not equipment, geography or time of day, what accounts for the difference between the four and the other 12?

### 8.
What fixes did 4.2 address?

### 9.
How could fix one be affecting misses and late-delivery?

### 10.
what might account for the 5 day lag in quiet tickets?

### 11.
how do we map the people reporting the tickets to the people experiencing the biggest issues from the data?

### 12.
Did all responders have tickets, and if not, who did and how many tickets?

### 13.
Pick one responder from the file who went quiet.  Show every week for that responder, how many times we pinged and how many they took.  Tell me what happened to this responder in plain english please.

### 14.
How else might we see how this affected Farlight compared with the other responders?

### 15.
what is one simple sentence to explain what happened to farlight?

### 16.
what is different about the four from the others?  Does the data prove that the misses affect their rank?

### 17.
what is the correlation between the pings and the miss rates?

### 18.
What is correlation between misses and receiving future pings?

### 19.
If I'd only asked for one number, not the rows, what would I have missd?

### 20.
Tell me what is the correlation and percentages of taken/missed/declined when the responder is the first choice or the second (or beyond) choice. Is the wait time 60 seconds for second pings as well?

### 21.
Why did Farlight and some other responders stop receiving pings altogether?
